About NASNARI

Leading construction and integrated concepts excellence in Nigeria

Our Story

NASNARI Integrated Concepts Limited was founded by a team of dedicated professionals with a clear vision to transform Nigeria's construction landscape. What started as a focused team of passionate engineers has evolved into a multi-disciplinary company serving diverse clients across residential, commercial, and infrastructure sectors.

Over the years, we've completed 150+ projects, served 80+ satisfied clients, and built a reputation for excellence, integrity, and innovation. Our commitment to quality workmanship, on-time delivery, and customer satisfaction has made NASNARI the trusted partner for major construction projects throughout Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and beyond.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To deliver exceptional construction and integrated concept solutions that transform visions into reality with integrity, innovation, and unwavering excellence on every project.

Vision

To be Nigeria's most trusted integrated construction partner, recognized for quality craftsmanship, sustainable development, and positive community impact.
